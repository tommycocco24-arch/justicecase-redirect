Free hornbooks and course texts

Study texts you can read free and legally, by course. Open textbooks and casebooks are current works their authors released under open licenses. Classic hornbooks are the original West Hornbook Series and similar treatises from before 1931, now public domain: great for how doctrines developed, but they state the law as of their year, so check anything you rely on against current sources.

Why isn't my hornbook here?

Current commercial hornbooks, Nutshells, and Examples & Explanations are copyrighted, so they can't be shared free. Everything here links to the book's own free, legal home: the Open Textbook Library (University of Minnesota) for open-licensed books, mostly published by CALI eLangdell Press, and the Internet Archive for public-domain classics. More free casebooks: H2O Open Casebook (Harvard Law School Library).

A study aid, not legal advice. Briefs are AI-generated and fact-checked against the court's opinion, but can still be wrong. Later history is checked only against free public sources, not a full citator. Always read the opinion and follow your school's honor code.
